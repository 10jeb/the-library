The Current administration topic covers President Trump's second term from January 20, 2025, built from official records through mid-October 2026: laws on GovInfo, orders in the Federal Register, Senate votes and Supreme Court dockets.

Questions cover both parties and stick to what the record shows. "Promise" questions compare a public pledge with what officially happened afterward.
