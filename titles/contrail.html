Fly a trainer, an airliner or a fighter through ring courses in four landscapes. Lift, drag, stalls, engines and landing gear are modeled from real aircraft numbers.

Controls

  • S↓ pull back, nose up
  • W↑ push forward, nose down
  • AD ←→ roll
  • QE rudder
  • RF throttle up / down
  • Shift afterburner (jet)
  • XZ flaps down / up
  • G landing gear
  • Space wheel brakes · speed brakes in the air
  • [] pitch trim (trainer)
  • C camera · M mouse yoke
  • P pause · Esc menu · U sound

Gamepads work too: left stick flies, triggers set throttle, bumpers move flaps, A brakes, B gear, Y camera.
