For learning only. Real illnesses rarely look like the textbook: symptoms overlap, many people have only some of them, and some serious conditions present atypically. This is not a diagnostic tool. If you or someone else has symptoms like these, contact a health professional, and call emergency services for sudden chest pain, stroke signs, trouble breathing or a severe allergic reaction.
